Look for:
- Injection (SQL, shell, template) and unvalidated input
- Broken authentication or missing permission checks
- Secrets or credentials in code, config or logs
- Sensitive data exposed in responses, logs or errors
- New dependencies with known vulnerabilities

A scanner finding is evidence to act on: confirm it in the diff and list it as a finding; one the results say master has too is pre-existing: note it, but it alone is no reason to reject. Never copy a secret value into security-review-{variation}.md or anywhere else: name its merge request, file, line and kind only.

Finish with one verdict for them all, as JSON: {"is-approved": true} if nothing must be fixed first, or {"is-approved": false} if security-review-{variation}.md lists something that must.
